Scope and limitations

FormationRescue removes one dancer, checks spacing and stage boundaries, and attempts shape-aware repair for supported patterns. It does not yet guarantee aesthetically ideal formations, collision-free transitions, or optimal movement across an entire routine. All choreography changes require captain review.
