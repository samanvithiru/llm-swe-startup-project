Scope and Limitations

This Alpha prototype supports single-dancer removal, baseline formation repair, and validation of spacing and stage boundaries. AI instructions are limited to supported repair preferences. The prototype does not yet optimize transitions across the entire routine or guarantee that different preferences produce different formations.
